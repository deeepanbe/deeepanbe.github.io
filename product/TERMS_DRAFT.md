Repository data and learning signals should be retained only for the period disclosed in the privacy policy. Provide deletion/export controls where technically feasible.

The service must not be used to deploy malware, steal credentials, exfiltrate secrets, evade security controls, or violate third-party terms.

Final commercial terms should specify billing, renewal, refunds, taxes, support, availability, privacy, data processing, acceptable use and dispute handling.
