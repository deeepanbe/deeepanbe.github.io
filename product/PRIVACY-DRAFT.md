# DJ AI Privacy Notice — Draft

DJ AI processes account, resume and job-search information only to provide requested services. The service should collect the minimum data needed, encrypt data in transit and at rest, restrict staff access, support export/deletion requests, and define retention periods before launch.

Resume and job-profile data is sensitive personal information. Do not use it for model training without a separate lawful basis and explicit product disclosure/consent where required.

Third-party platform credentials are not required for the manual-assist workflow. LinkedIn/Naukri access, if later offered, must use authorized APIs/partners and their current requirements.

A final jurisdiction-specific privacy notice must be reviewed by qualified counsel before paid launch.
