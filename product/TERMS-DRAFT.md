# DJ AI — Terms Draft

**Draft only; obtain qualified legal review before commercial launch.**

DJ AI provides software-assisted resume structuring, job matching, document generation and application assistance. Users remain responsible for the accuracy of their profile, documents and final application decisions.

DJ AI does not guarantee interviews, employment, compensation or selection outcomes.

Users must not provide credentials or secrets for platforms where automated access is prohibited. LinkedIn and Naukri workflows will use authorized APIs/partners or manual-assist flows requiring the user to take the final application action.

Users grant DJ AI only the permissions necessary to provide requested services. We will not sell personal profile data. Retention, deletion, subprocessors, security controls and jurisdiction-specific rights must be disclosed in the final privacy notice.

Abuse, fraudulent applications, impersonation and unlawful use are prohibited.

This document is a product/legal starting point, not legal advice.
