# DJ AI Revenue Model

## Product
DJ AI is positioned as an AI engineering assistant for individual developers and small teams, with a career automation module as an optional second product line.

## Unit economics
Track per account:
- AI/API cost
- compute cost
- storage cost
- successful agent tasks
- PR acceptance rate
- support cost

## Monetization experiments
Free: limited repository scans.
Developer: ₹499/month.
Pro: ₹1,499/month.
Team: ₹4,999/month.
Business: negotiated private deployment/API.

These are launch hypotheses, not guaranteed pricing. Increase limits/prices only after measuring retention and gross margin.
