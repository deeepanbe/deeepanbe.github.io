# Analytics Case Studies

This folder documents the portfolio case-study system used on the live site.

## Power BI Universal Analytics Dashboard

- Business problem: scattered sales, inventory, and operating KPIs slowed leadership review.
- Stack: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Oracle SQL, data modeling.
- Impact: unified KPI layer for faster executive reporting and drill-through analysis.
- Recruiter signal: BI development, stakeholder storytelling, semantic measures, and dashboard structure.

## Enterprise Analytics Project

- Business problem: raw operational files needed a structured path from ingestion to reporting.
- Stack: Azure Data Factory concepts, Azure SQL, Blob Storage, Python, Power BI.
- Impact: demonstrates analytics engineering thinking and source-to-dashboard design.
- Recruiter signal: data pipeline awareness, validation, and cloud analytics readiness.

## Customer Segmentation ML

- Business problem: marketing teams need clearer customer groups and retention signals.
- Stack: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, clustering, EDA.
- Impact: turns customer behavior into targetable segments.
- Recruiter signal: applied machine learning, analytical explanation, and business interpretation.

## BigQuery E-Commerce Analysis

- Business problem: order, customer, product, and payment data needed actionable revenue analysis.
- Stack: BigQuery, SQL, GCP, exploratory analysis.
- Impact: query-led insights across sales trends and category contribution.
- Recruiter signal: SQL depth, cloud warehouse familiarity, and business framing.

## Olist E-Commerce BI Dashboard

- Business problem: Brazil e-commerce order data needed a clear BI story across sellers, categories, geolocation, reviews, and logistics.
- Stack: Power BI, DAX, Python, Power Query, e-commerce operations data.
- Impact: 99,441 orders analyzed with a dashboard-ready model, DAX measures library, and processed data pipeline.
- Recruiter signal: real marketplace analytics framing, order lifecycle thinking, and enterprise-style BI storytelling.
