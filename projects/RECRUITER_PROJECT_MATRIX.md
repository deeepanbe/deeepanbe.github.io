## Interview positioning

The strongest positioning is: **experienced operations professional building a dedicated analytics career, with verified portfolio evidence across BI, SQL and Python.**

Do not describe the portfolio as commercial Data Analyst employment. Do not invent ROI, savings, client impact, prediction accuracy or production deployment where the portfolio does not document them.

## Recommended interview sequence

1. Explain the business problem.
2. Explain the dataset grain and quality checks.
3. Explain the transformation/modeling approach.
4. Explain KPI definitions and analytical logic.
5. Explain the dashboard or analytical output.
6. Explain the most useful insight and possible business action.
7. Explain limitations and what would be done next with production data.
